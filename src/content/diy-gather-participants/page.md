subheading1: How do I get the word out?
paragraph1: it's much easier to flip off in community than on ur own. the first step is to <u>find at least one other person who wants to help u bring this to life</u>.
paragraph2: work together to <u>recruit a group of 10–30 participants</u> to go offline together. set up a phone number, email address, or form where ppl can sign up. invite some friends to join you, and ask them to invite some of their friends.
paragraph3: flyer on utility boxes, in cafes, book shops, bars -- or wherever u can find a community bulletin board. table on a busy street corner. submit an invitation to local newsletters or e-mail listservs. try to <u>avoid using social media</u> ... u will be surprised by how many ppl come out of the woodwork! the medium is the message.
paragraph4: <u>cold call</u> ppl as they sign up. ask them about why they decided to take the plunge, their hopes & dreams for the month. make sure they're free on the date & time you've selected. share a bit about urself and what to expect from the meetups.
paragraph5: '<u>send a reminder</u> 1 week before and 1 day before with all ur deets: date, time, & location. try including a playful instruction like "look 4 the man in the sunglasses and give him the secret passcode LESS IS MO."'
subheading2: Where should we meet up?
paragraph6: <u>choose a day and time</u> that u and ur fellow organizers are available for 5 consecutive weeks. meetups typically last 1.5-2hours, and remember u will need at least a half hour on either end for set up and takedown!
paragraph7: <u>find a local venue</u> that will host u, and get to know the owners. Month Offline is a great way to patronize local businesses and support IRL community hubs. private rooms or relatively quite spaces at restaurants & bars tend to work best, but some groups meet outside at the park.
paragraph8: once you've found ur ppl and place, ur ready to start prepping for the meetups!
paragraph9: dumb.co is offering a $100 starter stipend to organizers to help cover the cost of getting ur cohort off the ground!
