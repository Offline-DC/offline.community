subheading1: Why dumb down?
paragraph1: most of us have learned the hard way that we need more than just self-control, grayscale screens, and private resolutions to reduce our screen time. ppl get the most out of MO when they <u>fully abandon their smartphones</u> for the month.
paragraph2: why? every device has an opinion about how we should be relating, attending, and spending our time -- an opinion expressed in the form of its hardware and software design. beginning in the '90s, a couple of companies decided it would be a good idea to try combining telephones and computers into one all-purpose pocket communicator. the dumbphone says nah. its intentionally-limited functionality leaves room for mind & body wandering, for fruitful friction, for glorious glitches.
subheading2: What are the dumbphone deets?
paragraph3: all dumbphones are welcome at Month Offline, but most ppl use the dumbphone 2 from dumb.co. the company was started by two of MO's founders <em>and</em> some of the very first participants. inspired by everything they learned during MO, they built a dumbphone that makes it easy (but not too easy) for ppl to flip off.
paragraph4: 'MO participants have a few options when it comes to acquiring a dumbphone:'
options:
  - <u>borrow a refurbished dumbphone 2</u> for free (with $10 shipping) and mail it back at the end of the challenge -- recommended for ppl who are testing the waters
  - <u>get a new dumbphone 2</u> for $20 + 4-month minimum phone plan -- recommended for ppl who want to dive right into dumb life
  - <u>bring a dumbphone they already own</u> or try an alternative from <a href="https://www.dumbphones.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Dumbphone Finder</a>
paragraph5: make sure participants order their phones at least 1 week before ur challenge begins, to allow enough time for shipping.
paragraph6: organizers will receive a free phone from dumb.co plus 3 months of free phone service. when u submit an <a href="https://tally.so/r/RGgD2p" target="_blank" rel="noopener">interest form</a>, we'll also share with u the (private) link where ppl can order a refurbished dumbphone.
